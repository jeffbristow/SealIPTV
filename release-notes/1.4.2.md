- M3U playlists: changing your playlist link, username or password keeps your favorites, watched marks, resume points and your own categories. Seal IPTV loads the new link straight away and tells you how many titles it kept.
- Switching a playlist to another provider: favorites and watched marks move to the same titles there when Seal IPTV can find them by name.

On the TV, Settings → About → Check for updates installs it. Windows updates by itself.
