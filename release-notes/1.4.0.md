- New in Collections: Disney, Netflix and Prime Video. Disney has Disney's movies (Walt Disney Pictures, Disney Animation, Pixar, Disney+ and Disney Channel films); Netflix and Prime Video have the movies and shows each one made. Only what your playlist has is shown, newest first or oldest first, and Netflix and Prime Video can show just movies or just shows.
- These collections keep up to date by themselves: the lists come from Wikipedia and are refreshed every week, so new titles appear without waiting for an update.
- A show is no longer matched to a collection's show of the same name that started years later.

On the TV, Settings → About → Check for updates installs it. Windows updates by itself.
