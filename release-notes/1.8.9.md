Screensaver when paused:
- When a movie or episode has been paused for 2 minutes with no button pressed, the screensaver comes on (the Seal IPTV logo moving on black), so a still picture can't burn into the screen. Any button brings the paused picture back and does nothing else.
- Fire TV and Google TV: in the player too. Windows: also when the player is full screen. Phones: the screen may go to sleep while paused.
- It follows **Settings → Playback → Screensaver** (on / off).

Learn intros (Windows):
- **Learn now** only looks at series and seasons not learned yet. Seasons that can't be read on Windows (AC-3 sound) or that the provider doesn't have are noted and not tried again each time. **Learn all seasons again** still goes over everything.
- The list in Settings shows those seasons with the reason.
