- Home starts with something to watch: a big spotlight of what you were watching, with ▶ Resume (or the newest movie). The numbers of channels, movies and series, and your account, are now in Settings → About.
- Much faster start-up on Fire TV and Google TV with a big playlist (on a Google TV with 190 000 titles: from about 30 seconds to about 12). The saved playlist is also smaller, which takes effect the next time it reloads.
- Recent searches: select the empty search box to search again for one of your last ones.
- On TVs, results show while you type in the search box.
- Voice search on Google TV: the 🎤 next to the search box, or the remote's search button.

This needs the new TV app: Settings → About → Check for updates installs it. Windows updates by itself.
