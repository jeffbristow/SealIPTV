- A start-up screen while your playlist loads: the Seal IPTV logo and a moving bar, so it's clear the app is working and not frozen. A big playlist can take a while on a TV.
- Settings can be locked with the parental PIN: Settings → Parental controls → "Ask for the PIN to open Settings". After the PIN, Settings stays open for as long as locked categories do (until you lock or close Seal IPTV, or for the time you chose); 🔓 Lock in the sidebar locks it again. The choice syncs to your other devices.
- Seal IPTV also runs on Google TV / Android TV (the same app as on Fire TV).

Windows and Fire TV update by themselves (Settings → About → Check for updates).
