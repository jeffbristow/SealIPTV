New: Seal IPTV for Android phones — `SealIPTV-Android-1.8.0.apk`. The same playlists, favorites, watched marks, sync and VPN guard as Windows and the TV, laid out for a phone:
- Tabs at the bottom: Home, Live TV, Guide, Movies, Series, and More (Sports, Pay-Per-View, Collections, Favorites, Watchlist, your categories, Settings).
- Categories open in a sheet with a box to find one. Tap a channel to watch it; press and hold anything for its menu.
- The player: tap for the controls; ☰ Channels, ▲ ▼ for the channel before / after; double-tap the left or right to skip 10 seconds, the middle to pause; drag on the right for volume, on the left for brightness; ⚙ for audio, subtitles, picture size and audio sync. Leaving the app while something plays keeps it in a picture-in-picture window.
- Setting up is quick: on the phone, choose "📲 Get it from another device". It shows a short code; on your PC or TV (already in sync) choose Settings → Sync → "📺 Connect a TV or phone", pick the phone and type the code. The phone joins your sync with your playlists and settings — nothing else to type.
- With the VPN guard on, nothing plays unless the VPN is connected, and the player closes if the VPN drops.

Windows and Fire TV / Google TV:
- Settings → Sync → "Connect a TV or phone" now finds phones too.
- If the settings file can't be read when Seal IPTV starts, it's kept aside instead of being replaced, so your playlists aren't lost.
