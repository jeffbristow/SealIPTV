- New TV Guide: channels down the side and the time across, starting with your favorite channels. Move along a channel to see what's on later, with the time and description at the top. Press OK (or double-click on Windows) to watch.
- The full TV guide from your provider is now used. For Xtream playlists it's found automatically from your login. Channels it doesn't cover still show the provider's own listings. Live TV and the player's channel list use it too.
- Playlists have a new "TV guide" choice: Automatic, a guide address of your own (XMLTV, for M3U playlists), or Off.
- TV: a movie or series opens with Play or Resume selected. On a series, moving onto a season shows its episodes, and → on an episode reaches its ✓ (watched) button.
- TV: pressing Favorite, ✓ or a season keeps your place instead of jumping elsewhere.

On the TV, Settings → About → Check for updates installs it. Windows updates by itself.
