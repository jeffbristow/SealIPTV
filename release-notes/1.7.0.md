Windows:
- Downloads: choose "⬇ Download" on a movie, ⬇ on an episode or "⬇ Download season" to watch without the internet. Downloads go on where they stopped after a dropped connection, a pause or a restart, and play here (or in VLC) with the same resume point and watched mark. See them under Downloads in the menu.
- Recordings: record a live channel now (⏺ Record in Live TV or in the player) or at a time, or record a programme from the TV Guide (right-click it). Recordings are under Recordings in the menu, as .ts videos that play here and in VLC. They happen while Seal IPTV is open; it keeps Windows awake while it records, and asks before closing if a recording is running or scheduled.
- Streams at the same time (Settings → Recordings & downloads, 1 by default): watching, each recording and a download each use one, so Seal IPTV never asks your provider for more than it allows. With 1, a download waits while you watch, and playing something while a recording runs asks first, because it stops the recording.
- Choose where downloads and recordings are saved (Videos\Seal IPTV by default).

Fire TV / Google TV: no changes in this version.
