- Remove something from Continue watching: when you open it from Continue watching, the "Pick up where you left off?" question now has "✕ Remove from Continue watching". You can also right-click (☰ Menu on the remote) a Continue watching card, or choose "✕ Remove" on the big panel at the top of Home. A series goes with all its episodes; it comes back if you watch it again. The resume point is kept, and the change syncs to your other devices.
