- Live TV on Fire TV / Google TV: a channel guide over the picture. Press OK or ← while watching: the channels of the category (or of the shelf / your own category you started from) with what's on now and next. ↑ ↓ and OK change channel, Back or → closes it.
- Type a channel number on the remote to go straight to it; 0 on its own goes back to the channel you watched before.
- Films and episodes on TVs: ← → skip 10 seconds; hold them to go faster, with a bar showing where you'll land.
- The ☰ Menu in the TV player is now one panel on the right: audio track, subtitles, speed, picture size (Fit, Zoom or Stretch, remembered), audio sync, next episode and another app.

This needs the new TV app: Settings → About → Check for updates installs it (Android asks once to allow Seal IPTV to install apps). Windows updates by itself.
