New: age ratings on every device (Windows, Fire TV / Google TV, Android phones, Ubuntu Touch).
- Movie and series posters show their US rating in a corner: G, PG, PG-13, R, NC-17 for movies; TV-Y through TV-MA for shows.
- The movie and series pages show the rating at the start of the info line, before the ★ score.
- Ratings come from TMDB, using the key in Settings → Home page & posters (the same key used for missing posters); without a key, only ratings your provider sends itself are shown. Each title is looked up once and remembered, and nothing is looked up while the VPN guard is holding the network back.
