- Give a channel, movie or series its own name inside one of your categories: for example "NBC" instead of "FL | Fort Myers | NBC WBBH" in Local Channels. Open the category, choose ✏️ Edit titles, then ✏️ Rename (or right-click / ☰ Menu → Rename). Everywhere else it keeps its name, and the new name syncs to your other devices.
- US local stations now show their network's logo: ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX, PBS, The CW, MyNetworkTV, Telemundo, Univision and ION. This works in Live TV, Favorites, Home and your own categories, and also for channels you added before. The networks' cable channels (FOX News, NBC Sports, PBS Kids …) and channels from other countries keep their own pictures.

Windows and Fire TV update by themselves (Settings → About → Check for updates).
