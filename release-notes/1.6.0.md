- Watchlist: choose "📌 Watchlist" on a movie or series (or in its ☰ Menu / right-click menu) to keep it for later. It's on Home under Continue watching and has its own page in the menu. A movie leaves the list once you've watched it; a series shows "New episodes" when the provider adds more. The watchlist syncs to your other devices.
- Sports: a new page with the games on now, later today and tomorrow, from your provider's event channels and the TV Guide of its sports channels. Filter by sport or by region; a game on several channels or feeds is one card, and you pick the feed.
- Pay-Per-View: a new page with the pay-per-view events on now and coming up.
- Home shows "Live sports now" and "Pay-Per-View now" rows; switch them off in Settings → Home page & posters.
