- New on Home: Trending movies and Trending series, showing what's popular on Netflix, Disney+, Apple TV, Hulu, Peacock, Paramount+, Prime Video and HBO Max that your playlist has. Under each poster it says where it's popular, like "Netflix #1" or "Hulu".
- The lists come from Netflix's own Top 10 and TVmaze. With a TMDB key (Settings → Posters & trending), movies popular on the other services are added too. These are the same lists for everyone: nothing about you or your provider is sent.
- Turn it off in Settings → Posters & trending.

On the TV, Settings → About → Check for updates installs it. Windows updates by itself.
