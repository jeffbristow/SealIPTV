New: Seal IPTV for Ubuntu Touch (preview) — `seal-iptv.jeffbristow_1.8.1_all.click`, for Ubuntu Touch 24.04. The same phone layout as the Android app, with your playlists, favorites, sync and the VPN guard:
- Works with a VPN set up in Ubuntu Touch's own network settings (WireGuard or OpenVPN, e.g. Proton VPN's WireGuard configuration): with "Only play when my VPN is on", nothing plays unless the VPN is connected, and the player closes if it drops. "Open ProtonVPN" opens the phone's VPN settings.
- Plays with the phone's own media service; when that can't play a stream (on some phones it can't open the sound output), the stream plays in the app's built-in player instead.
- "📲 Get it from another device" works here too: join your sync with a short code from your PC or TV.
- Install it by hand (OpenStore → "Install from file", or `click install` in developer mode). It doesn't update itself yet.

Android phones:
- Posters keep their shape on older Android System WebViews.
- Small screens: the welcome screen's buttons wrap instead of running off the edge.

Windows, Fire TV / Google TV: no visible changes in this version.
