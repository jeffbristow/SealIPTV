- Posters no longer disappear and come back when you move between Home, Favorites, Movies and Series. Some providers have a picture server that hangs for 20 seconds before failing; Seal IPTV now remembers those addresses and shows the replacement poster straight away.
- Fire TV: posters and logos are now saved on the device the first time they're shown (like on Windows), so they appear at once afterwards, also after restarting. This needs the new Fire TV app: Settings → About → Check for updates offers it after the page update.

Windows and Fire TV update by themselves (Settings → About → Check for updates).
