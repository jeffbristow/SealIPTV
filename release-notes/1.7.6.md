Fire TV / Google TV:
- Moving with the remote: ↑ and ↓ stay on the page (they no longer jump into the side menu at the bottom of a page) and stay in the side menu (they no longer jump out of it onto the page); → from the menu goes back to where you were on the page; ← at the start of the search box goes on to the menu.
- Closing a dialog (for example cancelling the PIN for a locked Settings) puts the selection back where it was.
- Live TV: a channel shows what's on as soon as you move onto it.
- TV Guide: a channel without a guide no longer scrolls the grid sideways; the times and "No guide for this channel" stay in view.
- The Newest / A–Z / My order buttons show the whole selection frame.

Windows:
- Settings → Categories is readable in a small window (the names no longer shrink to a letter, and the Live TV / Movies / Series tabs aren't hidden).
- A small window keeps room for the search box; the screensaver setting reads as one line.

Both: Home's "Live sports now" and "Pay-Per-View now" rows use wide cards, so event names fit.
