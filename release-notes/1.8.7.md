Learn intros only when you start it:
- Seal IPTV for Windows no longer looks for intros and end credits by itself. Reading episodes uses your provider connection, which another device may be using to watch at the same time.
- Start it yourself in **Settings → Playback → Skip intro and Next episode** with **Learn now**, when nobody is watching on another device. It still pauses while something plays on this PC, and **Stop** ends it at any time.
- What it learns still goes to your other devices through **Sync**.

Also:
- Episodes in AVI files can be read too (when their sound isn't AC-3).
