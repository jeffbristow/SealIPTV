Skip intro and Next episode, found by listening:
- While a series episode plays, Seal IPTV listens to its sound, and only to what you're already watching: nothing extra is streamed or downloaded.
- After you've watched two episodes of a series, it knows the series' intro and its end credits. From then on, a **Skip intro** button appears a few seconds into the intro, wherever it falls in the episode (a longer or shorter opening scene doesn't matter). A **Next episode** button appears when the end credits start.
- Remote: press OK while the button shows (Back hides it). Windows: click it, or press S or Enter.
- A new season with a new intro is learned too. Shows without a repeated intro or credits simply don't get the buttons.

Fire TV, Google TV and Android phones: this version comes as a new app (APK) to install.
