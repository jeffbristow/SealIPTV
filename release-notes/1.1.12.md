- Settings in sections: Playlists, Sync, VPN, Playback, Parental controls, Categories, Posters, Updates & history and About in a list on the left, one at a time on the right, each with a short status. On a TV, moving down the list shows each section; → or OK goes in, Back comes out.
- On TVs, on / off settings are switches that say On or Off, and OK on a choice slides the options in from the right instead of a drop-down list.
- On TVs, it's easier to see where you are: whatever is selected gets a green glow and grows a little. Back puts you on the poster you opened. Choosing a menu item takes you onto its page.
- On TVs, the sidebar shows only icons while you browse, which leaves more room for channels and posters. Press ← to open the whole menu.
- Fixed: after some dialogs, Enter (OK) could stop working until Seal IPTV was restarted.

Windows and Fire TV update by themselves (Settings → About → Check for updates).
