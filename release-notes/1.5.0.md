- Set up a TV from your phone: on the TV, choose "📱 Set up from your phone" (on the welcome screen, or in Settings → Playlists or Sync). Scan the QR code with your phone, then type your playlist login or sync server on the phone instead of with the remote. What the phone sends is encrypted with the code on the TV.
- Connect a TV from another Seal IPTV: on your PC or another TV that's already in sync, choose Settings → Sync → "📺 Connect a TV", pick the TV and type the code it shows. The TV joins your sync with all your playlists, favorites and settings, without typing an address or passphrase. (A device connected before this version asks for the passphrase once.)

This version needs the new Fire TV / Google TV app: on the TV, Settings → About → Check for updates installs it. Windows updates by itself.
