Skip intro and Next episode, learned ahead of time:
- Seal IPTV for Windows now finds each season's intro and end credits for the series on your **Watchlist** and in **your own categories**. It reads only the sound at the start and end of a few episodes per season, so you don't have to watch anything first.
- It happens by itself a few minutes after Seal IPTV starts, and twice a day after that, only for seasons it hasn't looked at yet. It waits while something is playing. You can also start it in **Settings → Playback → Skip intro and Next episode**.
- What it finds goes to your other devices through **Sync**: Fire TV, Google TV and phones get Skip intro and Next episode for those series without learning them first.
- The full intro is found, even when it's long. Skip intro now jumps to its real end.
- Episodes with AC-3 or E-AC-3 sound can't be read on Windows. Those seasons are still learned on the TV as you watch them.

Also:
- On Windows, Skip intro and Next episode now work for providers that send episodes from another server. Before, the player couldn't listen to them.
