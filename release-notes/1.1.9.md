- Your own categories: choose "＋ New category" in the menu (for example Kids or Date night), then put any channels, movies and series in it with the "＋ Category" button, a right-click, or ☰ Menu on the Fire TV remote. Each category gets its own place in the menu and a row on Home, and they sync between all your devices.
- Screensaver: after 5 minutes with nothing playing and nothing pressed, the Seal IPTV logo moves around a black screen. Any key, click or remote button ends it. Turn it off or change the time in Settings → Playback.

Windows and Fire TV update by themselves (Settings → About → Check for updates).
