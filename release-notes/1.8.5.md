Skip intro, more reliable and sooner:
- **Skip intro** now shows about 2 seconds into the intro, every time. Before, it could take several seconds longer, vary from episode to episode, or not show at all.
- Intros are learned much more reliably. The sound is now listened to in finer steps, so the same intro is recognised even when it starts at a slightly different moment in each episode.
- A series' real intro is learned, not a channel logo at the very start, or a scene that two episodes happen to share.
- When some episodes of a series have a short intro and others the full one, the button skips just the short one, or the whole one when it goes on. When the intro ends early, the button goes away.
- Skipping lands right at the end of the intro, not a few seconds into the episode.

Series are learned again from scratch with this version: after two episodes of a series, the buttons are back.

Fire TV, Google TV and Android phones: this version comes as a new app (APK) to install.
