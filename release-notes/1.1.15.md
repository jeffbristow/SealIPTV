- Posters and pictures from your provider show again on Google TV (and other TVs with a newer Android WebView), on Home, in Movies, Series and everywhere else.
- Continue watching shows the series' poster when an episode has no picture of its own.
- A picture that didn't load because the provider was slow or busy is tried again after 10 minutes instead of 6 hours.

This needs the new TV app: Settings → About → Check for updates installs it. Windows updates by itself.
