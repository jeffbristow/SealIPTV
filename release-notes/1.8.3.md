Faster and smoother on TVs, especially the Google TV (all devices get the fixes where they apply):
- Moving around with the remote is much quicker: the green ring no longer makes the TV lay out the whole page on every press (posters and buttons respond in about a third of the time).
- The green ring and glow show in full on Home's rows (they were cut off at the top and sides).
- Reloading a big playlist no longer freezes the remote for many seconds: the provider's lists are read and saved a piece at a time, and on a TV the automatic reload waits until the remote has been left alone.
- Pictures are kept at the size a TV shows them: Home uses about a quarter of the memory it did, so low-memory TVs slow down and stall far less.
- Movies, Series and Home open faster.
- The sidebar's numbers are what the pages list: hidden and locked categories and hidden other-language titles aren't counted.
- After the player closes, or a reload in the background, the remote stays on the same title instead of jumping to the top or the sidebar.
- Recent searches no longer fill up with half-typed words when typing with a remote.
- TV Guide: the channel line above the grid is no longer cut in half.

Fire TV, Google TV and Android phones: this version comes as a new app (APK) to install.
